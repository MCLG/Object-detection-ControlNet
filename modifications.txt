ControlNetHome/cldm/cldm.py 
    class ControLDM 
        get_input: handles the batch. Now handles the centroids and Gaussian density maps for control input
        on_after_backward: clipping gradients
        decode_first_stage_train: standard decode_first_stage but with gradient checkpointing
        compute_loss: called in p_losses and fetches the DM loss, then computes the control loss
        count_guidance_sampling: a ddpm method with classifier guidance. Depends on the loss type selected (attribute control_eval)
